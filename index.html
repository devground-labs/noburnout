Why NoBurnout

Because the best way to stay productive is to actually take a break.

Quick to start

Open the page and play. Nothing to install, no sign-up, no waiting on a launcher.

Easy to put down

Short rounds with no daily streaks or pressure. Play for five minutes, then get back to it.

Plays anywhere

Runs in your browser on desktop or mobile, with keyboard or touch controls.

Play with a teammate

Challenge a colleague on one keyboard, or play solo against the AI to blow off steam.

Built in the open

NoBurnout is open source and made by people who like a good break. Found a bug, got a game idea, or want to build one yourself? Your help makes it better for everyone.
